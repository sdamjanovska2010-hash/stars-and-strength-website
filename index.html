About Stars & Strength

Stars & Strength was founded by Sofija and Angjela, two students who believe that young people do not have to wait until adulthood to make a difference. We wanted to create an organisation that brings students, volunteers, schools, and local communities together around a meaningful cause.

Through fundraising, awareness campaigns, volunteering, and community projects, we aim to turn compassion into action. We hope to support children and families during difficult times while inspiring other young people to recognise the difference they can make.

We are still at the beginning of our journey, but we believe that every project, volunteer, and act of kindness can help us build something meaningful.
